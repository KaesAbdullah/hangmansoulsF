HOW TO PLAY

MECHANICS

WELCOME TO HANGMAN SOULS!
YOU ARE THE FIGURE ON THE LEFT, AND EACH ENEMY IS BOUND TO A HIDDEN WORD.
GUESS IT LETTER BY LETTER TO DESTROY IT.

EVERY WRONG LETTER LETS THE ENEMY STRIKE AND YOU LOSE HEALTH.
THE ENEMY HAS NO HEALTH BAR, BUT IT FALLS THE MOMENT YOU COMPLETE ITS WORD.
WORD HINT ON THE RIGHT ARE THERE TO HELP YOU GUESS.

ITEMS & SKILLS

THE FLASK HEALS 40 HEALTH.

THE DODGE HAS A 40% CHANCE TO AVOID THE NEXT ATTACK COMPLETELY.

NEW ITEMS AND SKILLS WILL BE UNLOCKED DURING YOUR JOURNEY!

STATUS EFFECTS

FROZEN: THE ENEMY WILL NOT ATTACK ON YOUR NEXT LETTER.

STUNNED: THE ENEMY WILL NOT ATTACK FOR 2 TURNS. A STUNNED ENEMY GLOWS YELLOW AND TREMBLES.
